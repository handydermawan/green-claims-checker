How ratings are decided

Claude answers yes/no questions about each claim. The rating comes from fixed rules in this application, so the same answers always give the same rating.

RatingRule
0 · High riskRelies on carbon offsets, or uses a broad or absolute term (“sustainable”, “recyclable”, “net zero”) with no evidence named, or is a future target with no plan or evidence.
2 · SupportedNo high-risk flag, and it gives a number, says what it covers, names evidence, states a baseline for any comparison and a plan for any target.
1 · Needs workEverything else: not misleading on its face, but missing a number, scope, baseline or evidence.
